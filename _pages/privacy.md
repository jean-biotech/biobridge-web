BioBridge is a free, student-led resource. This page explains, in plain language, what happens with your information when you use this site.

## What this site collects

BioBridge is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't run our own servers or databases, and we don't sell or share data with advertisers.

- **Hosting logs.** GitHub Pages, our host, may log basic technical information (like IP address and browser type) as part of normal web hosting. We don't have access to this beyond what GitHub provides.
- **The Application Reviewer tool.** If you use the AI-powered application reviewer on the Tools page, the resume and job description text you paste in is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate feedback, then returned to your browser. We don't store that text on our own servers, and we don't ask for your name or contact information to use the tool.
- **Outbound links.** Some pages link to newsletters, podcasts, courses, and other resources hosted by other organizations (the Learning Lab). Once you leave BioBridge, that organization's own privacy policy applies.
- **Email.** If you email us directly, we'll have your email address and whatever you wrote, like any email.

## What we don't do

We don't use tracking cookies, ad networks, or third-party analytics that build a profile of you. We don't sell any information to anyone.
